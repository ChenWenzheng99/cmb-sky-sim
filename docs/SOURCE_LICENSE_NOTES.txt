1. Scientific inputs and citations

mmDL convergence and extragalactic fields
  Provider: public NERSC mmDL archive.
  https://portal.nersc.gov/project/cmb/data/generic/mmDL/README
  Han, Sehgal and Villaescusa-Navarro (2021), Physical Review D 104, 123521.
  https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.104.123521
  Training-simulation context: Sehgal et al. (2010), ApJ 709, 920-936.
  https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/709/2/920
  The provider offers several frequency channels. This work selects the
  148 GHz tSZ, CIB and radio maps as the frequency-scaling references, together
  with the same-index convergence and kSZ maps. The upstream lensed CMB maps
  are not the CMB input branch described in main_revise.

CMB primary and remapping
  Primary spectra: CAMB 1.6.1, with the declared Sehgal2010 parameter completion.
  Lewis, Challinor and Lasenby (2000), ApJ 538, 473-476.
  https://doi.org/10.1086/309179
  Unlensed CMB sky realizations are drawn from these spectra. The implementation
  represents them harmonically and passes their coefficients, together with
  the deflection derived from the matched mmDL convergence, to lenspyx 2.0.0.
  The manuscript's unlensed-maps step describes the underlying sky realization;
  it does not imply that an intermediate unlensed FITS file is written.
  Reinecke, Belkner and Carron (2023), Astronomy & Astrophysics 678, A165.
  https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202346717

Galactic components
  PySM 3.4.4, presets d9 / s4 / f1 / a1, respectively thermal dust,
  synchrotron, free-free and anomalous microwave emission.
  Thorne et al. (2017), MNRAS 469, 2821-2833.
  https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stx949
  Zonca et al. (2021), Journal of Open Source Software 6(67), 3783.
  https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.03783
  Actual input-template URLs and SHA256 hashes are recorded in
  release_package/runtime_evidence/template_manifest.json.
  The generation record is evidence/galfg_new/Gal_FG_manifest.json.

Noise configurations
  Source: release_package/provenance/mpi_red_noise.py and the distributed
  SAT/LAT configuration examples. These are reference simulation parameters;
  no measured Ali instrument performance is inferred from the configuration.
  White and red angular-power terms define the map-level Gaussian noise model.

Pixelization
  Gorski et al. (2005), ApJ 622, 759-771.
  https://doi.org/10.1086/427976
